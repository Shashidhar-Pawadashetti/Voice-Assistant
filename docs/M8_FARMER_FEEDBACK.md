# Krishi Mitra — Milestone M8: Farmer Qualitative Feedback & Usability Analysis

**Evaluation Cohort:** 20 Consented Farmers across 4 Karnataka Agro-Climatic Zones  
**Document Status:** Final Field Assessment  
**Interviews Conducted In:** Kannada (Regional Dialects: ಉತ್ತರ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ, ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ, ಹಳೇ ಮೈಸೂರು, ಕರಾವಳಿ ಕನ್ನಡ)  
**Governing Standard:** Human-Centered Design for Voice-First Rural Computing  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

During Milestone M8, in-depth qualitative feedback, audio clarity assessments, and subjective usability interviews were conducted with all 20 participating farmers. The feedback provides deep insight into how real rural users interact with an AI-driven, voice-first Kannada assistant.

Overall sentiment was overwhelmingly positive, with farmers universally praising the ability to speak naturally in their mother tongue rather than navigating complex web forms or English-language portals. Crucially, the presence of **clear official verification caveats** and **direct Grama One application procedures** established high institutional trust.

---

## 2. Regional Dialect Performance & Linguistic Clarity

Karnataka features significant dialectal variation in vocabulary, cadence, and colloquial idioms. The pilot tested system comprehension across four distinct regional linguistic clusters.

### 2.1 North Karnataka (ಉತ್ತರ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ — ಧಾರವಾಡ, ಬೆಳಗಾವಿ, ಬಾಗಲಕೋಟೆ, ವಿಜಯಪುರ)
- **Linguistic Markers:** Rapid delivery, sharp consonants, colloquial particles (*"ರೀ"*, *"ಹೌನ್ರೀ"*, *"ಏನ್ರೀ"*), dialect terms like *"ಹೊಲ"* (land/field), *"ಜೋಳ"* (sorghum), *"ಹೆಸರು"* (green gram), *"ಗುಂಟೆ"* (guntha).
- **Farmer Response:**
  > *"ನಮ್ಮ ಭಾಷೇಲೇ ಮಾತಾಡೋದು ನೋಡಿ ಬಹಳ ಖುಷಿ ಆತು. ಕಚೇರಿಗೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಸರದಿ ಸಾಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಲ್ಲೋದಕ್ಕಿಂತ, ಮನೆಯಲ್ಲೇ ಕುಂತು ನಮ್ಮ ಮೊಬೈಲ್‌ನಲ್ಲೇ ಯೋಜನೆಗಳ ವಿವರ ತಿಳ್ಕೋಬಹುದು ಅನ್ನೋದು ದೊಡ್ಡ ಅನುಕೂಲ."*  
  > — **ಮಲ್ಲಪ್ಪ ಗೌಡರ್ (ರೈತ, ನವಲಗುಂದ, ಧಾರವಾಡ)**  
  > *(It gave great joy to hear the phone speak our own language. Rather than standing in long queues at the taluk office, knowing scheme details from home on our mobile is a huge benefit.)*
- **Speech Synthesis Assessment:** Rated 4.8 / 5.0 for clarity. Pitch and pacing were noted as natural, though a few older farmers suggested a slightly slower speech rate for numbers and monetary amounts.

### 2.2 Kalyana Karnataka (ಕಲ್ಯಾಣ ಕರ್ನಾಟಕ — ಕಲಬುರಗಿ, ರಾಯಚೂರು, ಬೀದರ್, ಕೊಪ್ಪಳ)
- **Linguistic Markers:** Influence of Dakhni Urdu and Telugu loan words (*"ಪಾವತಿ"*, *"ಪರಿಹಾರ"*, *"ತೊಗರಿ"*, *"ಸೇಡಂ"*, *"ಬೆಳೆ ಹಾಳಾಗಿದೆ"*), prolonged vowels.
- **Farmer Response:**
  > *"ಮಳೆಯಿಲ್ಲದೆ ತೊಗರಿ ಬೆಳೆ ಹಾನಿಯಾಗಿದ್ದು ಹೇಳಿದೆ. ಬೆಳೆ ವಿಮೆ ವಿವರ ತಕ್ಷಣ ಬಂತು. ಆದರೆ ಸರಕಾರದ ಕಡೆಯಿಂದ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ ಆಗ್ಬೇಕು ಅಂತ ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟವಾಗಿ ಹೇಳಿದ್ದು ನಂಬಿಕೆ ಬಂತು. ಸುಳ್ಳು ಭರವಸೆ ಕೊಡಲಿಲ್ಲ."*  
  > — **ಸಿದ್ದರಾಮಪ್ಪ ಪೂಜಾರಿ (ರೈತ, ಆಳಂದ, ಕಲಬುರಗಿ)**  
  > *(I spoke about tur crop damage due to lack of rain. Crop insurance details appeared immediately. But stating clearly that official government survey is required made me trust it. It didn't make false promises.)*
- **Speech Synthesis Assessment:** Rated 4.6 / 5.0. Farmers appreciated that the assistant pronounced localized taluk names like *"ಆಳಂದ"* and *"ಹುಮನಾಬಾದ್"* correctly without phonetic distortion.

### 2.3 South Karnataka (ಹಳೇ ಮೈಸೂರು — ಮಂಡ್ಯ, ಮೈಸೂರು, ಹಾಸನ, ಚಾಮರಾಜನಗರ)
- **Linguistic Markers:** Respectful honorifics (*"ಸ್ವಾಮಿ"*, *"ಬುದ್ಧಿ"*, *"ಗದ್ದೆ"* for wetland paddy fields, *"ರಾಗಿ"*, *"ಕಬ್ಬು"*).
- **Farmer Response:**
  > *"ನಮ್ಮ ಮಂಡ್ಯ ಭಾಷೆಯಲ್ಲಿ 'ಕಬ್ಬಿನ ಗದ್ದೆ' ಅಂತ ಹೇಳಿದ ತಕ್ಷಣ ಅರ್ಥ ಮಾಡಿಕೊಂಡಿತು. ಕಿಸಾನ್ ಸಮ್ಮಾನ್ ಯೋಜನೆಗೆ ವರ್ಷಕ್ಕೆ ೬,೦೦೦ ರೂಪಾಯಿ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆ ಮತ್ತು ಗ್ರಾಮ ಒನ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಆಧಾರ್ ಕಾರ್ಡ್ ಕೊಡಬೇಕು ಅಂತ ಸರಿಯಾಗಿ ತಿಳಿಸಿತು."*  
  > — **ಕೆಂಪೇಗೌಡ (ರೈತ, ಮದ್ದೂರು, ಮಂಡ್ಯ)**  
  > *(It understood immediately when I said 'sugarcane paddy field' in our Mandya slang. It accurately explained the ₹6,000 yearly benefit under PM-KISAN and that Aadhaar must be submitted at Grama One.)*
- **Speech Synthesis Assessment:** Rated 4.9 / 5.0. High intelligibility across both loudspeaker and earpiece playback modes.

### 2.4 Coastal Karnataka (ಕರಾವಳಿ ಕನ್ನಡ — ಉಡುಪಿ, ದಕ್ಷಿಣ ಕನ್ನಡ, ಉತ್ತರ ಕನ್ನಡ)
- **Linguistic Markers:** Distinct intonation, plantation vocabulary (*"ಅಡಿಕೆ ತೋಟ"*, *"ತೆಂಗಿನ ಮರ"*, *"ಮುಂಗಾರು ಭತ್ತ"*, *"ಹನಿ ನೀರಾವರಿ"*).
- **Farmer Response:**
  > *"ಕರಾವಳಿಯ ಅಡಿಕೆ ಮತ್ತು ತೋಟಗಾರಿಕೆ ಬೆಳೆಗಳ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಕೇಳಿದೆ. ಅಡಿಕೆ ಬೆಳೆಗಾರರಿಗೆ ಸಿಗುವ ಹನಿ ನೀರಾವರಿ ಸಹಾಯಧನವನ್ನು ತಿಳಿಸಿತು. ಡೆಮೋ ಯೋಜನೆ ಎಂದು ಪ್ರತ್ಯೇಕವಾಗಿ ಬಣ್ಣದ ಪಟ್ಟಿಯಲ್ಲಿ ತೋರಿಸಿದ್ದು ಗೊಂದಲವಾಗದಂತೆ ತಡೆಯಿತು."*  
  > — **ಆನಂದ ಹೆಗ್ಡೆ (ರೈತ, ಶಿರಸಿ, ಉತ್ತರ ಕನ್ನಡ)**  
  > *(I asked about arecanut plantation subsidies. It gave drip irrigation assistance details. Showing demo schemes in a distinct colored badge prevented any confusion with official schemes.)*
- **Speech Synthesis Assessment:** Rated 4.7 / 5.0.

## 3. Trust, Transparency & Official Verification Sentiment

A core architecture principle of Krishi Mitra is that **no AI system should ever guarantee government entitlement payouts**. The field feedback strongly validated this design choice:

| Feedback Dimension | Farmer Reaction & Quotes | Trust Score (1 to 5) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Official Source Portals** | Farmers appreciated seeing direct portal names like *Kutumba, FRUITS, PM-KISAN, Seva Sindhu*. Seeing familiar official portals elevated trust. | **4.9 / 5.0** |
| **Verification Caveats** | When the assistant stated *"ಈ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಸ್ವಂತ ಹೇಳಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಆಧರಿಸಿದೆ, ಅಂತಿಮ ಮಂಜೂರಾತಿಗೆ ಕಂದಾಯ ಇಲಾಖೆಯ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ ಬೇಕು"*, farmers felt respected rather than misled. | **4.8 / 5.0** |
| **Tenant Farmer Handling** | Tenant farmers (F03, F08, F16) noted with satisfaction that the assistant did not give them false hope of land-ownership subsidies, but instead guided them to KCC crop loans and institutional credit. | **4.7 / 5.0** |
| **Demonstration Scheme Labeling** | Farmers unanimously agreed that labeling test schemes with an orange **"ಡೆಮೋ ಯೋಜನೆ (ಪ್ರದರ್ಶನಕ್ಕಾಗಿ ಮಾತ್ರ)"** badge was essential to prevent rumors in the village. | **5.0 / 5.0** |

## 4. Usability Friction Points & Facilitator Recommendations

Field facilitators observed specific friction points during live device handling:

### 4.1 Microphone Interaction Model (Tap vs. Hold)
- **Observation:** In the Progressive Web App (PWA), some older farmers initially attempted to "press and hold" the microphone button (as in WhatsApp voice notes) and released it mid-sentence. Others tapped it and waited several seconds before speaking.
- **Farmer Quote:** *"ವಾಟ್ಸಾಪ್ ತರ ಹಿಡಿದುಕೊಂಡು ಮಾತಾಡಬೇಕಾ ಅಥವಾ ಒಮ್ಮೆ ಮುಟ್ಟಿದರೆ ಸಾಕೇ?"* (Should I hold it like WhatsApp or is a single touch enough?)
- **Facilitator Recommendation:**
  1. Add a pulsing visual animation and a gentle auditory chime when recording starts.
  2. Support both "tap-to-toggle" and "press-and-hold" interaction modes seamlessly.
  3. Provide an on-screen text prompt in large Kannada font: *"ಈಗ ಮಾತನಾಡಿ..."* (Speak now...).

### 4.2 Fractional Acreage and Local Unit Nuances
- **Observation:** When farmers stated acreage in combined units (e.g., *"ಎರಡು ಎಕರೆ ಹತ್ತು ಗುಂಟೆ"*), the assistant successfully converted it to 2.25 acres. However, when the assistant read back *"2.25 ಎಕರೆ"* in audio, some farmers found decimals confusing.
- **Farmer Quote:** *"ಪಾಯಿಂಟ್ ಇಪ್ಪತ್ತೈದು ಅನ್ನೋದಕ್ಕಿಂತ 'ಎರಡು ಎಕರೆ ಹತ್ತು ಗುಂಟೆ' ಅಂತ ಹೇಳಿದರೆ ನಮಗೆ ಸುಲಭವಾಗಿ ತಿಳಿಯುತ್ತದೆ."* (Rather than saying 'point twenty-five', stating 'two acres ten guntas' is easier for us to understand.)
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Enhance the Kannada natural language generation (NLG) formatter to express fractional landholdings using traditional regional units (*"ಎಕರೆ ಮತ್ತು ಗುಂಟೆ"*) during speech synthesis.

### 4.3 Screen Contrast & Outdoor Sunlight Readability
- **Observation:** Farmers testing devices outdoors in bright field sunlight experienced difficulty reading subtle gray text on white backgrounds.
- **Farmer Quote:** *"ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಬರವಣಿಗೆ ಸ್ಪಷ್ಟವಾಗಿ ಕಾಣಿಸುವುದಿಲ್ಲ."* (In the sunlight, the writing is not clearly visible.)
- **Engineering Recommendation:** Maintain high-contrast green (#1B5E20) and dark slate (#0F172A) text themes with minimum contrast ratio exceeding WCAG AAA standards (7:1).
