## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The objective of Milestone M8 is to conduct a strictly controlled, consent-based field usability pilot of the Krishi Mitra voice-first agricultural assistant across Karnataka. 

This pilot is **not a feature expansion milestone**. Rather, it validates:
1. Real-world farmer usability and accessibility using voice-first conversational interaction in Kannada.
2. Robustness of agricultural entity extraction (district, taluk, crop, acreage, land ownership, irrigation).
3. Correctness and auditability of the deterministic government scheme recommendation engine.
4. Privacy, consent lifecycle, and data protection enforcement under the DPDP Act 2023.
5. End-to-end system reliability, network disconnection resilience, and latency characteristics under rural 3G/4G network conditions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Regulatory and Legal Boundary:** Krishi Mitra is an advisory decision-support tool. A scheme recommendation generated by Krishi Mitra **must never be represented, implied, or treated as an approved government entitlement or administrative sanction**. All genuine schemes must clearly state official verification caveats, and all synthetic schemes must carry explicit `DEMO_ONLY` designations.

## 2. Pilot Cohort Design & Agro-Climatic Representation

The controlled pilot comprises a purposive cohort of **20 consenting farmers** representing the four primary agro-climatic zones of Karnataka. This cohort is sized to uncover qualitative usability friction, dialectal speech variation, and rule boundary conditions.

> [!NOTE]
> **Methodological Boundary:** This cohort does not claim statistical representativeness of Karnataka's entire farming population. It serves as a rigorous qualitative and operational pilot to identify usability barriers and architectural anomalies before wide-scale deployment.

### 2.2 Inclusion & Diversity Criteria
- **Land Holding Size:** Marginal (< 2.5 acres: 45%), Small (2.5 – 5.0 acres: 40%), Medium (5.0 – 10.0 acres: 15%).
- **Land Ownership:** Landowners (75%), Tenant/Sharecropper Farmers (25%). Tenant inclusion is mandatory to validate exclusion logic for owner-only subsidies.
- **Gender Representation:** Minimum 20% women farmers (4 of 20 participants).
- **Literacy & Tech Literacy:** Inclusive of non-literate and low-literacy smartphone users reliant exclusively on voice prompts.

## 3. Privacy, Consent & DPDP Act 2023 Compliance Protocol

Under India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)**, every participant must be provided with an unambiguous, purpose-specific, revocable consent notice before any data processing occurs.

### 3.1 DPDP Privacy Notice (Kannada & English)

#### Kannada Master Text (ಸಮ್ಮತಿ ಪತ್ರ):
> **ಕೃಷಿ ಮಿತ್ರ — ರೈತರ ವೈಯಕ್ತಿಕ ಮಾಹಿತಿ ರಕ್ಷಣೆ ಮತ್ತು ಸಮ್ಮತಿ ಪತ್ರ (DPDP Act 2023)**  
> 1. **ಉದ್ದೇಶ:** ನಿಮ್ಮ ದೂರವಾಣಿ ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ, ಕೃಷಿ ಭೂಮಿಯ ವಿವರ, ಮತ್ತು ಬೆಳೆ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು ನಿಮಗೆ ಸೂಕ್ತವಾದ ಸರ್ಕಾರಿ ಸೌಲಭ್ಯಗಳನ್ನು (ಯೋಜನೆಗಳನ್ನು) ತಿಳಿಸಲು ಮಾತ್ರ ಬಳಸಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.  
> 2. **ಧ್ವನಿ ಗೌಪ್ಯತೆ:** ನಿಮ್ಮ ಧ್ವನಿ ಮುದ್ರಿಕೆಯನ್ನು ಕೇವಲ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಮಾತನ್ನು ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳಲು ಬಳಸಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ. ಸಂಭಾಷಣೆ ಮುಗಿದ ನಂತರ ಮೂಲ ಆಡಿಯೋ ಫೈಲ್ ಅನ್ನು 15 ನಿಮಿಷಗಳಲ್ಲಿ ಸಂಪೂರ್ಣವಾಗಿ ಅಳಿಸಲಾಗುತ್ತದೆ.  
> 3. **ಮಾಹಿತಿ ಹಂಚಿಕೆ ನಿರ್ಬಂಧ:** ನಿಮ್ಮ ಒಪ್ಪಿಗೆಯಿಲ್ಲದೆ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಖಾಸಗಿ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು ಯಾವುದೇ ಮೂರನೇ ವ್ಯಕ್ತಿಗೆ ಅಥವಾ ವಾಣಿಜ್ಯ ಸಂಸ್ಥೆಗೆ ನೀಡುವುದಿಲ್ಲ.  
> 4. **ಹಿಂಪಡೆಯುವ ಹಕ್ಕು:** ನೀವು ಯಾವುದೇ ಸಮಯದಲ್ಲಿ ನಿಮ್ಮ ಖಾತೆಯನ್ನು ಮತ್ತು ನಿಮ್ಮ ಎಲ್ಲಾ ಮಾಹಿತಿಯನ್ನು "ಖಾತೆ ಅಳಿಸಿ" (Delete Account) ಆಯ್ಕೆಯ ಮೂಲಕ ರದ್ದುಗೊಳಿಸಬಹುದು.  
> *ನೀವು ಈ ಷರತ್ತುಗಳಿಗೆ ಸಮ್ಮತಿಸುತ್ತೀರಾ? [ಹೌದು / ಇಲ್ಲ]*

#### English Translation:
> *Krishi Mitra Farmer Privacy & Consent Notice:*
> 1. **Purpose:** Your phone number, land acreage, and crop details are processed solely to identify relevant government agricultural assistance schemes.
> 2. **Audio Privacy:** Your voice recordings are processed in memory to understand your intent. Ephemeral audio files are cryptographically protected and automatically purged within 15 minutes.
> 3. **No Commercial Sharing:** Your data is never sold, shared, or exposed to unauthorized third parties or commercial entities.
> 4. **Right to Withdraw & Erase:** You hold the unconditional right to export your data or permanently delete your account and personal history at any time.
> *Do you consent to these terms? [Accept / Decline]*

### 3.2 Ephemeral Audio Lifecycle & Retention Rules
1. **Zero Raw Audio Long-Term Persistence:** Audio uploaded during voice turns is stored in an ephemeral, owner-isolated directory (`.runtime/pilot_audio`).
2. **Time-To-Live (TTL):** Hard TTL is enforced at 900 seconds (15 minutes). An automated background cleaner unlinks expired WAV files.
3. **Owner Isolation:** Retrieval of audio files requires an authenticated JWT whose farmer ID matches the audio file's ownership metadata. Cross-farmer IDOR/BOLA attempts return HTTP 403 Forbidden.

## 4. Facilitator Onboarding Guidelines & Role Boundaries

Field facilitators are local agricultural extension assistants and community coordinators who facilitate device onboarding while strictly preserving the integrity of the farmer's unassisted voice interaction.

### 4.1 Do's for Facilitators
- **Read Consent Aloud:** Read the DPDP consent notice aloud in the farmer's local dialect before opening the application.
- **Let the Farmer Speak:** Allow the farmer to press the microphone button, hold the device, and speak naturally in their own words.
- **Explain Disclaimers:** Reiterate that Krishi Mitra is an AI guide and not a government office. Official documents must be submitted at the Grama One or Raitha Samparka Kendra.
- **Log Unprompted Confusion:** Record exact moments where the farmer paused, repeated words, seemed confused by system prompts, or asked for clarification.

### 4.2 Don'ts for Facilitators (Strict Guardrails)
- **NEVER Speak for the Farmer:** Do not speak into the microphone on behalf of the farmer.
- **NEVER Promise Entitlements:** Never say "This app will give you ₹6,000" or guarantee loan waivers.
- **NEVER Bypass Privacy Screens:** Do not click "Accept Consent" without the farmer's explicit verbal agreement.
- **NEVER Overwrite Farmer Answers:** If the farmer reports acreage in local units (e.g., 'ಗುಂಟೆ' or 'ಬಿಗಾ'), let the conversational dialogue handle or flag the unit rather than manually converting it on screen.

## 5. Support Helpline, Issue Escalation & Withdrawal Protocol

### 5.1 Dedicated Field Pilot Helpline
- **Toll-Free Pilot Support Desk:** `1800-425-KM-PILOT` (Mon–Sat, 07:00 – 19:00 IST)
- **On-Call Engineering Escalation:** Platform SRE and Lead Agronomist paging rota.
- **Facilitator Field WhatsApp/Telegram Channel:** Dedicated channel for real-time incident logging and network outage coordination.

### 5.2 Participant Withdrawal & Data Erasure Protocol
Every participant retains an absolute right under Section 6 of the DPDP Act 2023 to withdraw from the pilot at any point with zero negative consequences.

1. **Self-Service Erasure:**
   - The farmer accesses the Settings / Privacy screen.
   - Clicks **"ನನ್ನ ಖಾತೆ ಅಳಿಸಿ" (Delete My Account)**.
   - Enters their password and confirms with the prompt phrase `DELETE MY ACCOUNT`.
   - The backend synchronously revokes active tokens, scrubs personal identity hashes, unlinks conversation transcripts, and anonymizes recommendation records.
2. **Facilitator / Helpline Erasure:**
   - If the farmer calls the helpline or requests verbal withdrawal, the facilitator triggers an authenticated privacy deletion request on the farmer's behalf with two-factor authorization.
   - A cryptographic deletion audit receipt is logged in `app_audit_events`.

## 6. Pilot Usability Evaluation Tasks (The 11 Field Tasks)

Each farmer in the cohort executes eleven standardized field tasks:
1. **Task 1: Registration, Login & DPDP Consent:** Self-service registration with phone number, secure password, and verbal/UI DPDP consent acceptance.
2. **Task 2: Starting and Resuming Conversation:** Starting a fresh session and retrieving active conversation context.
3. **Task 3: Kannada Voice Recording:** Submitting spoken query in native dialect via 16kHz WAV voice turn.
4. **Task 4: District and Taluk Recognition:** Accurately identifying the farmer's geographical administrative jurisdiction.
5. **Task 5: Crop Identification:** Mapping local crop terminology (e.g., ತೊಗರಿ, ಅಡಿಕೆ, ಸಜ್ಜೆ) to canonical crop entities.
6. **Task 6: Land Area and Ownership Classification:** Correctly classifying acreage and distinguishing owner vs. tenant status.
7. **Task 7: Spoken Correction and Negation Handling:** Updating a previously stated slot (e.g., changing land from 2 acres to 3 acres) via voice negation.
8. **Task 8: Scheme Recommendation & Kannada Explanation:** Receiving explainable, deterministic recommendations with verified official-source links.
9. **Task 9: Authenticated Audio Response Playback:** Listening to synthesized Kannada audio output with owner token verification.
10. **Task 10: Network Interruption & Idempotent Retry:** Handling simulated 3G/4G network dropouts with client turn ID idempotency.
11. **Task 11: Privacy Data Export & Account Deletion:** Downloading personal data JSON and verifying complete account deletion.
