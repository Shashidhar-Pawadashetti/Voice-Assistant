# Krishi Mitra — Milestone M8: Farmer Usability Feedback & Consent Protocol Template

**Document Status:** Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)  
**Evidence Classification:** `DOCUMENTED_PROCEDURE` (Specification for field execution; zero participant claims)  
**Governing Laws & Standards:** Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023; ISO/IEC 25010 Usability Standard  
**Target Deployment:** Assisted Field Trials (N = 10–15 Consented Participants per Cohort)  

---

## 1. Scope & Research Objectives

This document establishes the official, auditable procedure for collecting, anonymizing, and analyzing real farmer feedback during Krishi Mitra assisted field trials in Karnataka.

### Core Objectives:
1. **Linguistic Intelligibility:** Evaluate whether rural Kannada speakers of diverse agro-climatic dialects understand synthesized TTS audio without cognitive strain.
2. **Domain Accuracy:** Measure whether farmer utterances (crops, landholding, land tenure, taluks) are correctly recognized and canonicalized.
3. **Institutional Trust & Transparency:** Verify that farmers clearly understand that Krishi Mitra provides informational guidance and that all government scheme benefits require official revenue/departmental survey and verification.
4. **Physical Interaction Ergonomics:** Observe outdoor sunlight screen usability, network interruptions, and microphone interaction models (tap-to-toggle vs. press-and-hold).

## 3. Participant Anonymization & Data Sanitization Protocol

To uphold research ethics, prevent data leakage, and ensure statutory compliance under the DPDP Act 2023:

1. **Pseudo-Random Identifier Assignment:**
   - Every participant is assigned a non-sequential, randomly generated code: `KM-P26-[DISTRICT_CODE]-[3_DIGIT_RANDOM]` (e.g. `KM-P26-DWD-842`).
   - The master linkage table mapping real names/phone numbers to participant codes is stored strictly offline in an encrypted physical drive locked by the Field Research Director.
2. **PII Scrubbing from Transcripts:**
   - Real names mentioned in speech must be redacted using standard token replacement: `[REDACTED_NAME]`.
   - Survey numbers, Aadhaar numbers, and bank details mentioned by farmers must be excised immediately: `[REDACTED_SURVEY_NO]`.
   - GPS locations are rounded to taluk level; exact farm coordinates are never logged in research artifacts.
3. **Public Repository Invariant:**
   - No audio recordings, unhashed phone numbers, or signed consent scans shall ever be committed to git repositories or public issue trackers.
   - All shared reports must contain aggregated statistical metrics or de-identified, non-traceable illustrative design quotes.

## 5. Standardized Five-Task Facilitated Evaluation Protocol

Facilitators must observe silently without leading, coaching, or speaking on behalf of the farmer. All facilitator actions are restricted to handing the test device to the farmer and instructing them to speak naturally.

| Task # | Task Description | Farmer Prompt (Kannada) | Observed Criteria | Success Condition |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **T1** | **Greeting & Initiation** | *"ಕೃಷಿ ಮಿತ್ರವನ್ನು ತೆರೆದು, ಧ್ವನಿ ಗುಂಡಿ ಒತ್ತಿ ಮಾತನಾಡಲು ಪ್ರಾರಂಭಿಸಿ."* | Taps mic, speaks greeting in native dialect. | Assistant generates clear spoken Kannada greeting within 2.5s. |
| **T2** | **Agricultural Fact Input** | *"ನಿಮ್ಮ ಊರು, ಜಮೀನಿನ ವಿಸ್ತೀರ್ಣ ಮತ್ತು ಬೆಳೆಯ ಬಗ್ಗೆ ಮಾತನಾಡಿ."* | Mentions district/taluk, acres/guntas, and primary crop. | Assistant extracts facts correctly without misidentifying location or crop. |
| **T3** | **Spoken Correction** | *"ಹಿಂದೆ ಹೇಳಿದ ಜಮೀನಿನ ಅಳತೆ ಅಥವಾ ಬೆಳೆ ತಪ್ಪಾಗಿದೆ ಎಂದು ಹೇಳಿ ತಿದ್ದಿ."* | Utters spoken correction (*"ತಪ್ಪಾಯಿತು..."*, *"ಬದಲಾಗಿ..."*). | Assistant updates specific slot, confirms change, and preserves other facts. |
| **T4** | **Scheme Audio Comprehension** | *"ಸಹಾಯಕ ಶಿಫಾರಸು ಮಾಡಿದ ಯೋಜನೆಯ ವಿವರವನ್ನು ಕೇಳಿ ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳಿ."* | Listens to synthesized explanation on speaker. | Farmer can state scheme name and primary benefit in own words. |
| **T5** | **Verification Caveat Recall** | *"ಈ ಯೋಜನೆಯ ಹಣ ತಕ್ಷಣ ಸಿಗುತ್ತದೆಯೇ ಅಥವಾ ಅಧಿಕಾರಿಗಳ ಪರಿಶೀಲನೆ ಬೇಕೇ?"* | Tests understanding of advisory vs. entitlement. | Farmer correctly states official verification/survey is required. |

## 7. Semi-Structured Qualitative Interview Guide

Facilitators should ask these open-ended questions and log the farmer's verbatim thoughts (translated or original Kannada):

1. **Dialect & Pronunciation:**
   - *"ಯಾವುದಾದರೂ ಪದ ಅಥವಾ ಊರಿನ ಹೆಸರು ವಿಚಿತ್ರವಾಗಿ ಅಥವಾ ತಪ್ಪಾಗಿ ಉಚ್ಚಾರವಾಯಿತೇ?"*  
     *(Did any word or place name sound strange or mispronounced?)*
2. **Speed & Volume:**
   - *"ಸಹಾಯಕ ಮಾತನಾಡುವ ವೇಗ ಹೇಗಿತ್ತು? ಸಂಖ್ಯೆಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಹಣದ ಮೊತ್ತ ಹೇಳುವಾಗ ವೇಗ ಜಾಸ್ತಿ ಅನ್ನಿಸಿತೇ?"*  
     *(Was speech speed appropriate? Did reading numbers or monetary amounts feel too fast?)*
3. **Trust & Skepticism:**
   - *"ಖಾಸಗಿ ಕಂಪನಿಗಳಿಗಿಂತ ಸರಕಾರಿ ಕಚೇರಿಯ ಗ್ರಾಮ ಒನ್ ಕೇಂದ್ರಕ್ಕೆ ಹೋಗಿ ಅರ್ಜಿ ಸಲ್ಲಿಸಿ ಎಂದು ಹೇಳಿದ್ದು ನಿಮಗೆ ಹೇಗೆ ಅನ್ನಿಸಿತು?"*  
     *(How did you feel about being directed to Grama One centers rather than commercial intermediaries?)*
4. **Outdoor Usability:**
   - *"ಹೊಲದಲ್ಲಿ ಬಿಸಿಲಿನಲ್ಲಿ ಮೊಬೈಲ್ ನೋಡುವಾಗ ಪರದೆಯ ಮೇಲಿನ ಅಕ್ಷರಗಳು ಮತ್ತು ಬಣ್ಣಗಳು ಕಾಣಿಸಲು ಕಷ್ಟವಾಯಿತೇ?"*  
     *(Was reading text on screen difficult under direct field sunlight?)*

## 9. Scheme Domain Expert Verification Panel Rubric

Before any feedback cohort is signed off as valid for agricultural guidance:
- A qualified agricultural extension officer, KVK scientist, or retired joint director of agriculture must independently audit the session traces.
- The expert verifies that recommendations generated matched state and national agricultural guidelines (e.g., proper fertilizer subsidy caveats, PMFBY cut-off dates, tenant eligibility bars).
- Form must be signed and dated with the officer's registration/employee ID.
